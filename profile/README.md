# CDTA Digital Services

Welcome to the CDTA Digital Services GitHub organization.

This organization contains the source code, shared components, infrastructure, and supporting assets for customer-facing digital services developed for the Capital District Transportation Authority (CDTA).

Our focus is the design, development, integration, and long-term support of secure, scalable, and accessible digital products that support riders, customers, and public-facing business operations.

## What We Build

CDTA Digital Services supports the development and maintenance of:

* Public websites and web applications
* Rider-facing digital tools and self-service experiences
* Shared UI components and design systems
* Public APIs and service integrations
* Mobile and responsive web experiences
* Content platforms and digital communications tools
* Customer-facing operational and service applications

## Core Principles

Our work is guided by a few simple principles:

* **Rider First**
  Build for clarity, accessibility, speed, and ease of use.

* **Secure by Default**
  Security, privacy, and governance are built into every layer.

* **Platform Over Point Solution**
  Prefer reusable services, shared components, and maintainable patterns.

* **Accessible and Inclusive**
  Public digital services must be usable by everyone.

* **Operationally Sustainable**
  Solutions must be supportable, observable, and maintainable over time.

## Engineering Standards

Repositories within this organization are expected to follow established standards for:

* Secure development practices
* Code review and branch protection
* Accessibility and usability
* Documentation and maintainability
* CI/CD and deployment controls
* Dependency and vulnerability management
* Logging, monitoring, and operational support

## Repository Structure

This organization may include:

* Product and application repositories

* API and integration services
* Infrastructure and deployment templates
* Documentation and standards
* Design and experience assets

## Collaboration

This organization supports collaboration between CDTA, implementation partners, and approved vendors contributing to customer-facing digital initiatives.

Contribution expectations, development standards, and repository-specific guidance are documented within each project.

## About CDTA

The Capital District Transportation Authority (CDTA) provides mobility solutions throughout New York’s Capital Region, connecting customers to transit, mobility, and digital services across the communities we serve.

For more information, visit [cdta.org](https://www.cdta.org).
